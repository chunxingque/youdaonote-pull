因为学习方法的变化而重来，学习数学



That Euclidean geometry is the geometry of physical space, that it is the truth
about space, was so ingrained in people's minds that for many years any contrary
thoughts such as Gauss's were rejected. The  mathematician Georg Cantor spoke of
a law of conservation of ignorance. A false conclusion once arrived at and
widely【was？】 accepted is not easily dislodged and the less it is understood the more
tenaciously it is held.
