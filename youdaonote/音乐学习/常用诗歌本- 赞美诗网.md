| 我要真誠 |
| - |
| 我要真誠，莫負人家信任深；我要潔淨，因為有人關心；我要剛強，人間痛苦纔能當；我要膽壯，奮鬥纔能得勝，我要膽壯，奮鬥纔能得勝。我要愛人，愛敵也愛淪落人；我要施贈，心誠，義重，財輕；我要虛懷，不忘我身多弱點；我要向上，學主榜樣助人，我要向上，學主榜樣助人。 |


| I would be true（英文版本） |
| - |
| I would be true, for there are those who trust me;I would be pure, for there are those who care;I would be strong, for there is much to suffer;I would be brave, for there is much to dare.I would be brave, for there is much to dare.I would be friend of all－the foe, the friendless;I would be giving, and forget the gift;I would be humble, for I know my weakness;I would look up, and laugh, and love and lift.I would look up, and laugh, and love and lift. |
