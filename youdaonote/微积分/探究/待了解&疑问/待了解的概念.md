It has been claimed that formalists, such as David Hilbert (1862–1943), hold that mathematics is only a language and a series of games.

The main opponent was the intuitionist school, led by L. E. J. Brouwer, which resolutely discarded formalism as a meaningless game with symbols (van Dalen, 2008).
