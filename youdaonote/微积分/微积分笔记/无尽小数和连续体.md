in 1585, Simon Stevin published La Thiende, giving a detailed discussion of the decimal representation of “fractions” (quotations marks from Wilder).



There are other approaches to the theory of irrational numbers beyond

those already mentioned or described. For example, Wallis in 1696 had

identified rational numbers and periodic decimal numbers. Otto Stolz

(1842-1905), in his Vorlesungen iiber allgemeine Arithmetik,1* showed that every

irrational number can be represented as a nonperiodic decimal and this

fact can be used as a defining property.
