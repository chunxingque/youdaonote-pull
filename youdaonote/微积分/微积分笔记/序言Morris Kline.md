举例说明这种方法并不是特立独行、前无古人

The use of an intuitive approach in the first treatment of a subject is not an innovation.

Arithmetic is learned intuitively in elementary school and then the logic of it is learned gradually

through the work in algebra. Geometry is learned intuitively in junior high school and then the formal

deductive approach is presented in senior high school. A difficult subject such as the calculus,

therefore, should certainly be introduced by an intuitive approach.
