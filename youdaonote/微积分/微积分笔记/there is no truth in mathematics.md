数学理论是建立在人类有限经验之上而发展起来的普遍适用的规律，说“有限经验”是因为谁都不可能检验完天地万物是不是都符合这个规律。

数学理论是建立在人类有限经验之上而发展起来的，所以数学理论揭示的仅仅只是在一个局域内的真实状况而已。总而言之，数学理论并不能放之四海皆准，它仅仅只是在一个局域内靠谱的准则而已。

先有Euclidean geometry

the predictions in the mathematical theories of astronomy, mechanics, optics, and hydrodynamics were in remarkably accurate accord with observation and experiment.Mathematics, then, provided a firm grip on the workings

of nature, an understanding which dissolved mystery and replaced it by

law and order. Man could pridefully survey the world about him and

boast that he had grasped many of the secrets of the universe, which in,

essence were a series of mathematical laws. The conviction that 

mathematicians were securing truths is epitomized in Laplace's remark that

Newton was a most fortunate man because there is just one universe

and Newton had discovered its laws.



Among the branches of 

mathematics, Euclidean geometry was most venerated, not only because it was

the first to be deductively established but also because for over two

thousand years its theorems had been found to be in perfect accord

with the physical facts.

以至于人们相信数学，尤其是Euclidean geometry是大自然秘密的揭示，它就是真理，

That Euclidean geometry is the geometry of physical space, that it is

the truth about space, was so ingrained in people's minds that for many

years any contrary thoughts such as Gauss's were rejected.

但是后来发现的non-Euclidean geometry竟然也能描述客观世界这点，给所有的人极大地震撼，人们对Euclidean geometry的固有观念不得不被改变，并致使人们郑重思考数学的本质（之前并不是没想过，但是这一次non-Euclidean geometry的发现带给了这个问题足够的重视），也认识到

It became clear that mathematicians had adopted

axioms for geometry that seemed correct on the basis of limited

experience

也就是数学理论的有先天的不足，数学并不是这个世界真实规则



数学起源于现实，数学理论是建立在人类有限经验之上而发展起来的普遍适用的规律，说“有限经验”是因为谁都无法保证没有公理（数学基于公理）外的例外状况（除非是造物者，否则谁都无法保证自己检验完了所有情况，只能保守地说在这个范围内普遍是这样的），所以现实中出现与数学理论相悖的状况也不是不可能，但这时候意味着旧理论的不足和新理论存在的契机，这么看来只有经历才能确实告诉我们数学理论对于何种情况适用，也就是我们无法保证数学理论适用于任何情况。

所以数学理论并不是客观真实世界秘密的揭示，其只能在一定范围内普遍有效适用的规则。

To accept the fact that mathematics is not a 

collection of diamonds but of synthetic stones, after the centuries of brilliant

successes in describing and predicting physical phenomena, would be

hard for anyone and especially for those who might be blinded by

pride in their own creations.



觉得数才能反映客观世界的想法也被当头棒喝

The sharpest attack on the truth of arithmetic came from Hermann

von Helmholtz (1821-1894),he considered the main

problem in arithmetic to be the justification of the automatic application

of arithmetic to physical phenomena. His conclusion was that only

experience can tell us where the laws of arithmetic do apply. We cannot

be sure a priori that they do apply in any given situation.

Each arithmetic is designed to

represent some class of phenomena of the physical world. 

可见算法也不是这个世界的真实描述，只是用来解决一个类型问题的规定，世间一切之现象未必都符合这一规定.



实践告诉我们1+1等于2对于两份1L的水的体积相加是适用的，在没有实践检验的时候谁都无法保证1+1等于2对于一份1L的水和一份1L的酒精的体积相加仍然是适用的

Only experience can tell us where ordinary arithmetic applies to

any given physical phenomena. Thus one cannot speak of arithmetic as

a body of truths that necessarily apply to physical phenomena. Of

course, since algebra and analysis are extensions of arithmetic, these

branches, too, are not bodies of truth.



Thus the sad conclusion which mathematicians were obliged to draw

is that there is no truth in mathematics, that is, truth in the sense of

laws about the real world. The axioms of the basic structures of

arithmetic and geometry are suggested by experience, and the structures as

a consequence have a limited applicability. Just where they are

applicable can be determined only by experience.



人类只是在有限范围内总结规律，应用在熟悉的范围内，不小心应用在范围外了发现错了，才能产生新的探索。这么看来数学就未必是这个世界的真实揭示了，只是一堆还算有用的规律啦

对不证自明的公理的接受就依赖于经历感受



人类在大千世界里通过经历去试图摸清这个世界的真理，然而再怎么摸索，谁都无法保证说自己已经摸清楚了这个世界的全部真理（除非是造物者，否则谁都无法保证自己检验完了所有情况，只能保守地说在这个范围内普遍是这样的），这就是先天的限制，所以说人类无法确保科学能适用于一切情况早已是命中注定的了。
