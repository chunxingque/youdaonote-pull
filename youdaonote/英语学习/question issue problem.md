较为普通的常用的question，大问题用其他两个



question

noun

1

[C] 问题；疑问 

a sentence, phrase or word that asks for information





C] ~ (of sth)

（待讨论或处理的）事情；议题；课题 

a matter or topic that needs to be discussed or dealt with





 issue

- 

N-COUNT 重要问题;议题;争论的问题 

An issue is an important subject that people are arguing about or discussing.

Agents will raise the issue of prize-money for next year's world championships...

经纪人将提出下年度世锦赛奖金的问题。

- 2

N-SING 首要事项;要点;问题的要害 

If something is the issue, it is the thing you consider to be the most important part of a situation or discussion.



problem

noun

1

棘手的问题；难题；困难 

a thing that is difficult to deal with or to understand

the economic problems of the inner city

逻辑题；数学题 

a question that can be answered by using logical thought or mathematics

mathematical problems
