I admire my uncle very much. He was raised in a poor rural family, as an elder brother, he was the first to be admitted by a university, thus set a good example to his brothers, he also tutored them in schoolwork, finally all his brothers got into universities, it is an unprecedented event that all children from a poor rural family got into universities among surrounding villages at that time. A few years later, he became the headmaster of a primary school, because some poor students could not afford the tuition, he didn’t want them to give up their study due to poverty, so he applied lands from the superior to plant and harvest vegetables and crops with these students to make money to pay for the tuition. Later in life, he was promoted as the headmaster of a high school, he was not only responsible for his work and teaching but also required other teachers to do so, which improved the teaching quality a lot so that more and more students in that school got admitted by various universities. That is, many students’ lives were changed by the education provided by my uncle and all the teachers along with him.
