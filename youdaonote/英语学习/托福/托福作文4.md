Do you agree or disagree with the following statement?

Parents should encourage their children to do their school work independently rather than help them with their school work.





Every child needs to go to school to acquire knowledge to prepare for the future, all of them are bound to encounter difficulties in their study, some parents think it is necessary to help their children with their schoolwork, while others not. This essay will discuss whether parents should help their children with their schoolwork.

Some parents think it is necessary to help their children to overcome difficulties in school work, for their children at home cannot get help from their teachers anymore, and if they cannot finish the homework before the next school day, they might be penalized by their teachers. Also, children’s bad habits are liable to be noticed by their parents and then get rectified, since with so many students in a class, it is hard for most children to get personal attention from their teachers, let alone get rid of their bad habits with the help of teachers. Therefore, children may study well with the help of parents.

However, it is also beneficial for a child to do his school work independently. First, he would develop the ability of self-study, after all, one of the aims of education is to cultivate the ability of studying independently. Once a child encounters a difficulty in his homework, and he cannot get help from parents, the situation could cause him to concentrate more in the class, or ask help from their teachers and classmates, which is good for developing their social skills. On the contrary, students who often get help from their parents may not get opportunities to develop these skills,  while children without parents’ help would learn abilities, such as self-study and sociability, earlier than those not.

Regarding whether parents should help their children with their schoolwork, we see pros and cons from discussions above, so it is not advisable that parents always help their children on their schoolwork, since this could hinder children from obtaining the benefit of study independently. In my opinion, parents should think twice before helping their children with their schoolwork.





Children are likely to meet with problems during their study, there are various reasons for this: a lack of concentration in class, teachers did not well explained the concept, and so on.
