Do you agree or disagree: Nowadays it’s easier tomaintain good health than it was in the past?



Thanks to the rapid development of science and technology in recent years, medical treatments and related facilities have also made significant progress, therefore some people think nowadays it is easier to maintain good health than it was in the past, while I hold a neutral point.

 

Admittedly, with the medical technology becoming more and more developed, many serious diseases that could not be cured before can be healed now. In addition, more and more people take physical examinations at least once per year, so insidious diseases within body can be eliminated before causing great suffering or damage. Thus in terms of these two factors, it is easier to maintain good health today.

 

However, economy develops more or less at the cost of environmental pollution, so our food, air, and water are likely to be polluted, which in turn harms our health and induces new diseases that are hard to cure. Besides, some medical treatments are too expensive so only a few people could afford it. Furthermore, there are more entertainments than before, such as social networks, movies, and online games, which require people to sit down to play or enjoy, so there is a tendency that people exercise less than before, which also induces diseases to modern people, so in terms of this point of view, I am also inclined to think people  in the past were a bit healthier than those of today because of more exercise they did. Although medical technology has advanced a lot in recent years, new diseases also emerges, thus it is not that easy to keep healthy as one might think today.

 

From what has been discussed above, we may draw the conclusion that it is hard to say maintaining health is easier today than before, although medical technology advances a lot, but pollution and unhealthy habits also induce new diseases that are hard to cure. I suggest that all of us exercise more and eat healthily to keep healthy（补充自己的观点，加长文章字数）.
