The passage talks about audience effects which means that individuals' work is affected by their knowledge that they are visible to others. When we know we're being watched as we perform some activity, we tend to increase the speed of doing it. The professor used two examples to demonstrate this. In the example of tying shoes, the group of students with the knowledge of being observed tied their shoes much faster than the other group. In the example of learning to type, people who are conscious of being observed typed a lot faster than they would if they were alone, but they also tend to make more mistakes at the same time, that is another common behavior.

The two examples basically explained the meaning of audience effects. （最后一句是总结句，考生可以根据自己的回答时间选择性舍弃。）
