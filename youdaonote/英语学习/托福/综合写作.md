passage 观点

when professors share their ideas with television audience, the professors' importance as scholars is enhanced. 

 

leads to an improved reputation for the university. And that improved reputation in turn leads to more donations for the university and more applications from potential students.

 

From professors on television, by contrast, viewers get a taste of real expertise and insight.

 

 

lecture观点

a professor who appears on TV tends to get the reputation among fellow professors of being someone who is not a serious scholar

 

being on TV can take a lot of a professor's time

 

What the networks want is the academic title, not the intellectual substance.

 

 

 

The lecture casts doubt on the point in the reading passage that it is beneficial for professors to appear on TV. The passage says professors who appear on TV can enhance their reputations in the academic fields, while the lecture refutes this with the opinion that TV appearances may undermine professors' serious impression and thus lead these professors unimportant roles in their academic fields and could hardly get research grants. The lecture also stated that TV appearances of professors is also at the cost of reducing their time on research and meetings with students. Besides, the lecture states that TV is not suitable for spreading serious knowledge, which is contrary to the opinion in the passage.

抄文中句子的问题

 

The lecture refutes the opinion in the passage that television appearances of professors are of great benefit to the professors themselves as well as to their universities and the general public.

Firstly, the lecture says a professor who often appears on TV could be thought as an entertainer rather a serious scholar by fellow professors, the behavior could undermine his academic reputation and even reduce his opportunities of attending academic meetings and getting research grants.

Another point of view expressed by the lecturer is that frequently TV appearances also takes a professor a lot of time, so his time on research and students also decreases.

Finally, although the reading passage asserts that viewers will get real expertise and insight from professors on television, the lecture maintains that what the TV networks want is the academic title, not the intellectual substance. What a professor could do on a TV appearance is usually to spread some basic knowledge on a specific topic, serious knowledge is often not needed, so his job in this case could be done as well by a reporter who has done some homework, which makes the professor’s TV appearance rather a waste of time than a necessity of spreading deep and thoughtful knowledge.

 

 

 

 

The passage introduced three reasons why professors should appear on TV: gaining reputation for themselves, for the college, and to educate the general public. However, the lecture disagrees.

Professors who appear frequently on TV are not generally viewed as a serious scholar. As a result, those professors will receive less invitation to attend academic conferences or less likely to receive research grant. This seriously hinders the development of the professor's research career.

The fact that professors who frequently appear on TV also has negative effect on students and the university. Appearing on TV takes a lot of time to prepare, including preparing for the material, transportation time, and even time to dress up, while these precious times could have been spent on teaching, researching and helping students.

Professors appearing on TV doesn't usually help on educating the general public. The TV network is not interested in having the professor explaining the intellectual substances of their researches. Rather, they are interested in having them explain some basic background information or history. This type of information can be readily presented by a serious reporter who has done his work properly.

Because of the above reasons, it is highly questionable whether professors’ appearances on TV has any advantage. In fact, it could bring negative effect to themselves and the universities they teach in.
